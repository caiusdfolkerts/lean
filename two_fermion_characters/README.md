# Initial coefficients and graded characters of two-fermion coinvariants

For the type A quotient over a characteristic-zero field, the coefficient ideals describe the initial ideal, and the admissible boxes give their Hilbert functions. At diagonal elements of GL₂, the character in each polynomial degree is a sum over these boxes. On the full quotient the trace is the bivariate Eulerian polynomial evaluated at one plus each diagonal entry.
