# Multiplication on the weight-graded relative Koszul complexes

For a finite simplicial poset and a downward closed subposet, the comparison between the barycentric and original relative Koszul chain groups preserves multiplication on the associated graded for the weight filtration. The two products restrict exterior multiplication with coefficients in the respective face-ring ideals. The formula holds in every pair of internal degrees, homological degrees and weights. This is the chain-level part of the multiplicative comparison in Theorem 1.2.
