# Products in the associated graded algebra of two-fermion coinvariants

For a positive number of variables over a characteristic-zero field, polynomial multiples of pure exterior color tensors span the associated graded algebra. Their multiplication corresponds to multiplying the polynomial factors and the assembled exterior tensors in the original algebra, then projecting to the initial quotient.
