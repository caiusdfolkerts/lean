# Elementary-parameter fibers of untruncated Griffin rings

Theorem 7.1 gives a product of tensor products of Springer rings for every elementary-parameter fiber after scalar extension to an algebraic closure. The companion selects the split-field algebra equivalence, the algebraic-closure equivalence with canonical root data, and the exact algebraic-closure factor count. The Challenge states the existence of the equivalences. Solution uses the retained explicit equivalences.
