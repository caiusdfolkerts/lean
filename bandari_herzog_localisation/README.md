# The Bandari–Herzog localisation conjecture

A nonzero monomial ideal is polymatroidal exactly when all its monomial localisations have linear resolutions, including at the graded maximal ideal. Local equigeneration suffices for ideals of intersection type. The minimum local generator degree is supermodular under local equigeneration, and the final criterion characterises polymatroidality by local equigeneration and the degrees of quotient socles.

The sole paper is [the supplied manuscript](paper.pdf). The four targets in [Challenge.lean](source/Challenge.lean) correspond in order to Theorem 1.1, Theorem 1.2, Lemma 2.1 and the closing socle criterion. [Solution.lean](source/Solution.lean) specialises the existing proofs.

Proof sources are retained from [the verified development](https://github.com/caiusdfolkerts/research/tree/921727a9d6ecd21cb68951c9c07787ddfb8c5ee2/polymatroidal_ideals).
