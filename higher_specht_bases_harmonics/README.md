# Simultaneous harmonic decompositions

The companion selects the simultaneous decomposition, grading, irreducibility and local finiteness assertions of Theorems 5.2 and 6.6. The Challenge uses the ordinary apolar coefficient pairing and states existence of a single family of irreducible subrepresentations for each set of ambient parameters. The witness formulas and the module isomorphisms that identify the Specht shape are outside the comparison.
