# Equivariant rank-selected cohomology of the relative weight filtration

In homological degree i and internal degree j, homogeneous relative Koszul homology has a filtration reaching the full space by level 2dj+1. In weight n its associated graded is the product of the rank-selected reduced relative cohomology groups of the indicated degree and weight. The same comparison intertwines every order automorphism preserving the subposet.
