# Barycentric invariance of relative Tor

For a finite simplicial poset and a downward subposet, the associated graded of the degree-j piece of the i-th relative parameter Tor module is linearly equivalent to the direct sum of reduced relative cohomology over the rank sets of total degree j. A rank set S contributes in cohomological degree |S| − i − 1. The coefficient ring may be any commutative ring.

Theorem 1.2 in the attached paper gives the relative formula. The source declaration is FourPapers.Universal.theorem_1_2_relative.
