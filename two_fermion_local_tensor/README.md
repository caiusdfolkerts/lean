# Exterior-bigraded tensor decomposition of local two-fermion quotients

At every support point of separated paired two-fermion data over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, the local quotient is isomorphic to the signed tensor product of its homogeneous cluster algebras. The isomorphism identifies the exterior components in every bidegree.

The [result map](map.json) identifies the selected statements and their source in the proof of Theorem 7.2.
