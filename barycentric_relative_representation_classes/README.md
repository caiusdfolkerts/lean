# Representation classes in the relative Tor formula

Over every field, including modular characteristic, the class of homogeneous relative Koszul homology in the Grothendieck group of finite-dimensional representations is the sum of the classes of the rank-selected reduced relative cohomology groups. Both actions are induced by order automorphisms preserving the downward closed subposet. The equivariant inclusion of homogeneous Koszul homology identifies the left side with the corresponding internal degree of derived Tor.
